About

I started out patching a payment integration that failed every third settlement, and I have been drawn to that kind of problem ever since. Most of my work is in the seams: idempotency, retries, backfills, the migration nobody wants to own. I write the runbook before the launch and I keep the postmortem short.

I like small teams with clear ownership, code review that argues about the right things, and dashboards that somebody actually reads. Outside work I mentor two backend cohorts a year and maintain a small Go library for queue consumers.
